Common questions

How does the free week work?

Everything in Hushpuddle is open for seven days from your first download. After that, a one-time unlock keeps everything. There is no subscription. You can see how many days are left under For grown-ups.

How do I restore my unlock on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Hushpuddle, and tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. An unlock also stays yours after deleting and reinstalling the app.

Does Hushpuddle need the internet?

No. Every idea and game works offline. The App Store is only needed at the moment you unlock or restore.

How do I choose a different age guide?

Tap the grown-ups button at the top of the home screen, or change next to the age guide. Age guides are a starting point; choose whatever fits your child today.

Where do the ideas come from?

Every idea is original and informed by guidance from organizations such as the AAP, CDC, Head Start, and ASHA. The full list of sources is in the app under For grown-ups, then Sources. Listing a source does not imply its endorsement.

Is Hushpuddle a developmental assessment?

No. These are play ideas, not tests or advice about your child’s development. If you have concerns about development or a loss of skills, talk with your child’s health professional.

How do I erase my information?

Open For grown-ups and tap Erase saved Hushpuddle information. Deleting the app also removes everything it saved on your phone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
