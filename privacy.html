Hushpuddle

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short: Hushpuddle does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. What you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

To work, Hushpuddle saves a few things in the app’s own storage on your device:

  • the child’s name or nickname, if you choose to enter one
  • the age guide and place you choose
  • your favorites, which ideas you have played, and any optional check-ins
  • when your free week started and whether you have unlocked the app

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. If your phone’s backups are turned on, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. Hushpuddle has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools, and it shares no data with third parties.

Purchases

Unlocking Hushpuddle is a purchase through the App Store. Apple processes it, and we never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app only learns whether an unlock exists so it can open for you. Apple’s privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Hushpuddle is made for grown-ups to read and use with the children they care for. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Erasing your information

Open For grown-ups and tap Erase saved Hushpuddle information, or delete the app. Either one removes what the app saved about your child. A purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
